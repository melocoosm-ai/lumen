# Lumen Privacy Policy (desktop)

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Lumen is a desktop web browser for Windows and macOS. **Lumen does not collect, sell or share any
personal data, and sends nothing to its developer.**

## What stays on your computer

Like any browser, Lumen's web engine keeps website cookies, cache and site storage in your user
profile folder so sites work as expected. This stays on your computer and is removed when you
uninstall Lumen. Lumen itself keeps no browsing history and no account.

## What Lumen does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics, advertising or tracking code
- No crash reports or usage statistics sent to the developer
- No access to your camera, microphone or location unless a website asks and you allow it

## Websites you visit

When you open a website, your computer connects directly to that site. The site and services it
uses may collect information under their own privacy policies. Searches typed into the address bar
go to DuckDuckGo (<https://duckduckgo.com/privacy>), which does not track searches.

## Children

Lumen does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
